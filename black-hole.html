Binary black hole merger

The holes begin 1.5 AU apart, with the smaller one to the right. Their 0.25 and 0.125 AU horizon radii imply 12.7 and 6.3 million solar masses. Planets start their usual mean Sun distances beyond the outer edge of the binary, with their former solar-orbit speeds. The inspiral follows a leading-order gravitational-wave estimate; planetary paths use Newtonian gravity. Light bending near two holes, merger, ringdown and the disks are visual approximations, not a numerical relativity solution.
